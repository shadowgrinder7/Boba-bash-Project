Call of Duty Mobile: From Rookie to Legendary

\Welcome to our guide on how to improve your skills in Call of Duty Mobile! Nowadays, it is much easier to reach legendary rank in multiplayer. You can follow these simple steps: 1. Improve your HUD Settings: Customize your HUD settings to suit your playstyle. Adjust the size and position of buttons for better control, watch HUD settings of leaderboard players for same. 2. Master the Maps: Learn the layout of each map, including common camping spots and high-traffic areas. This knowledge will help you anticipate enemy movements and plan your strategies accordingly. You can use private matches or bot lobbies for same. 3. Optimize Your Loadouts: Experiment with different weapons, attachments, and perks to find the best combination for your playstyle. Consider using loadouts that complement your strengths and cover your weaknesses. Check for season meta weapons and perks for same. 4. Practice Your Aim: Spend time in the training grounds or aim trainers to improve your accuracy and reaction time. Focus on headshots and learn to control recoil for better performance in battles.
